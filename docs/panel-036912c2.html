Ad Source

Personal Only: just your own ad from the Ad Content tab.
AdMob Only: a real Google AdMob banner instead — you earn per view/click, but you don't control what shows.
Both: alternates between your ad and an AdMob banner as people switch between screens in the app.

This is independent of the Active toggle on the Ad Content tab — AdMob keeps running even with your personal ad switched off, and vice versa.

About AdMob banners

Google-served banner ad
(rendered natively by Android, not shown here)
AdMob banners are a native ad slot pinned to the top of the screen, not a styled card — they won't carry the app's theme border or scroll with the page, and Google requires that (ads must stay visibly identifiable as ads). App ID and ad unit ID are configured in the app's own code (android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml and www/adMob.js), not here.

GitHub Connection

Generate one at github.com → Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Fine-grained tokens. Scope it to only this repository, with Contents: Read and write permission. Note: fine-grained tokens expire (often in 30-90 days) — if you start getting "Bad credentials," generate a fresh one here.
